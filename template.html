How to read this, and what it does not say

Seven days, not thirty

NASA's open FIRMS feeds cap at a 7-day window. Extending to 30 days needs a free FIRMS MAP_KEY and the area API, which accepts a 10-day range per request — three calls to /api/area/csv/{KEY}/VIIRS_SNPP_NRT/-25,33,40,63/10/{date} stitched together. Everything on this page is labelled by its true window; nothing is extrapolated.

The map frame spans 25°W–40°E, 33–64°N. That excludes % of detections, which are still counted in every figure but fall outside the drawn area.

Detections are not fires

A 375 m pixel flags a thermal anomaly. One fire generates many detections across multiple overpasses, and gas flares, industrial heat and crop burning all register. Clustering reduces this but does not eliminate it.

Radiative power is not burned area

FRP measures energy emitted at the moment of overpass. It indicates intensity, not extent. For burned area you need post-fire optical mapping — Sentinel-2 dNBR — or the official EFFIS perimeters.

Gaps are invisible

Thick smoke and cloud block detection, and each satellite sees a given point roughly twice daily. A fire that ignited and was contained between overpasses does not appear here at all. Absence of detections is not evidence of absence of fire.
